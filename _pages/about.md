Hi, I'm Kobi. I completed my BEng in Mechanical Engineering from Imperial College London in 2024. During undergrad I interned at Morgan Stanley and did research in RL at the Aerial Robotics Lab ([Hauf et al., “Learning tethered perching for aerial robots,” ICRA 2023](https://spiral.imperial.ac.uk/entities/publication/e776fe14-5a82-4e26-81c6-38fd0ee0a582)) and the [LSDS Group](https://lsds.doc.ic.ac.uk/). 

During summer 2024 I trained [world models](https://x.com/kobi_kelemen/status/1820002131736465772) as a member of the [Entrepreneur First](https://www.joinef.com/) program where I was offered $125k+ in pre-seed funding. I then did an AI Residency at [K-Scale Labs](https://www.kscale.dev/) where I worked with the awesome [Dr. Paweł Budzianowski](https://budzianowski.github.io/) to train a VLA with an experimental video model backbone. 

In early 2025 I joined [Irreverent Labs](https://supermodel.ai/) where we built real time video models for synthetic character generation. I built the agentic backbone for ['Trump'](https://mrpresident.ai/). Shortly after I got the itch to build general purpose robots, so I co-founded [General Bionix](https://generalbionix.com/), backed by Founders Inc and top angels. [Here's](https://x.com/real_vaishak/status/1922744605109350635) our launch vid. I trained VLAs ([1](https://x.com/real_vaishak/status/1903197039875461450), [2](https://x.com/real_vaishak/status/1903576835675578504)), organized a robotics [hackathon](https://x.com/real_vaishak/status/1927435606730350966) attended by 140+ people, and built our [data collection infra](https://x.com/kobi_kelemen/status/1972042356972310571) (among many other things).
